|date_format:'%d.%m.%Y': a format with % codes runs strftime(), deprecated since PHP 8.1 and removed from Smarty 5. Without %, date_format uses date().

The fix rewrites %Y %y %m %d %e %H %M %S %p %% to Y y m d j H i s A % (%M is minutes, so it becomes i). A format with a locale token (%A, %B, %j …) or with literal letters is reported without a fix: letters are format codes in date(), so 'Le %d' cannot be rewritten mechanically.

Not reported on cores older than 2.5.11, whose Smarty always used strftime().
