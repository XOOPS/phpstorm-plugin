In PCRE, $ matches before a final newline. preg_match('/^[a-z]+$/', $name) accepts "script\n", and the in_array() / === comparison that follows does not recognise it.

Reported when the pattern is a single-quoted literal ending in an unescaped $ without the D or m modifier and the subject variable is compared exactly later in the same scope, with no reassignment in between ($file = substr($file, 0, -4) compares something else). The fix replaces that $ with \z.
