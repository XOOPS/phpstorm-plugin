<{includeq}> and <{foreachq}> were XOOPS Smarty 2 plugins and no longer exist; the fix renames them to <{include}> and <{foreach}>.

<{php}> and <{include_php}> do not compile on Smarty 3 and later. They have no fix: move the code into the PHP page and assign the result to the template.

Not reported on cores older than 2.5.11.
